[color=#cc9933]The right spell, potion, weapon, or item — exactly when you need it.[/color]

Huginn is an SKSE plugin that watches how your fight is going and keeps your hotkeys stocked with whatever suits the moment — the right spell, potion, weapon, or item, already on the key, before you have to go digging for it in the menus.

It also learns as you play. Huginn notices what you actually reach for in each kind of situation and starts offering that sooner. And it never acts on its own: it puts the item on the key, you decide whether to press it.

Huginn provides a HUD widget showing the rolling state of each hotkey.

[line]
[size=4][b]Why[/b][/size]

Huginn is an attempt to solve inventory and spell selection without the player having to remember whether they have a potion, spell, or item for the job. Ideally Huginn abstracts away the inventory and ...

No... Why Huginn? Oh, that's because [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huginn_and_Muninn]Huginn is the raven that sits on Odin's shoulder bringing knowledge and gossip.[/url] That, and there are already one too many mods named Mimir.

[size=3][b]Install[/b][/size]

Huginn is entirely self-contained with its own rendering pipeline. Install the dependencies, then install Huginn using your favorite mod manager.

[size=3][b]Upgrading[/b][/size]

If you are uninstalling or upgrading, you might want to clear what Huginn has learned first: type [font=Courier New]hg reset weights[/font] in the console, or press [b]Reset Learned Weights[/b] in Huginn's dMenu panel. Huginn stores its learned preferences in the SKSE co-save.

[size=3][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]

Huginn can be installed and uninstalled at any time. Just delete or disable the mod in your mod manager.

[size=4][b]Configuration and Usage[/b][/size]

Huginn reads three files, each with a distinct job:

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]Huginn.ini[/font] in [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/[/font] — slots, pages, keybindings, scoring, Wheeler[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Huginn_Overrides.ini[/font] in [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/[/font] — classification fixes for modded items and spells[/*]
[*]dMenu [font=Courier New]Huginn.ini[/font] in [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/dmenu/customSettings/ini/[/font] — widget appearance and debug options[/*]
[/list]

[size=3][b]Huginn.ini[/b][/size]

[b]Configuration Location:[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/Huginn.ini[/font]

The majority of the recommendation algorithm is configured from this file. It is re-read every time you load a save, and can be hot-reloaded in game with [font=Courier New]hg reload[/font].

[quote]A note before you tune. The defaults below are chosen for steady keys: once something lands on a key, Huginn prefers to leave it there rather than chase the newest best pick. You can retune Huginn to react faster, but the keys will then change more often with newer recommendations; tune it the other way and it gets calmer but slower to catch up with lagging recommendations. The settings that decide this are under Steadiness — [SlotLocker] below. This is mostly user preference.[/quote]

[b][color=#cc9933]Basics[/color][/b]

Huginn is configured as [b]slots[/b], grouped into [b]pages[/b], with [font=Courier New][Keybindings][/font] tying a slot to a key. [font=Courier New][Page0.Slot0][/font] is driven by [font=Courier New]iSlot1Key[/font] — slots are numbered from 0, keys from 1 — meaning Huginn keeps that hotkey stocked with its current best pick, swapping it out as the situation changes. Alternatively, WheelerAPI can be used to surface each page as a Wheeler wheel and each slot as a wheel entry.

You can create up to 10 pages containing up to 10 slots per page. [font=Courier New][Pages][/font] sets [font=Courier New]iPageCount[/font]; each [font=Courier New][PageN][/font] sets [font=Courier New]sName[/font] and [font=Courier New]iSlotCount[/font] in the ini.

[b][color=#cc9933]Slot configuration[/color][/b]

Every slot takes the same six settings:

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]sClassification[/font] — What is allowed in this slot — see the list below[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]bWildcardsEnabled[/font] — Let Huginn occasionally offer something outside its usual pick, so it can find out what else you like[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]bOverridesEnabled[/font] — Whether an emergency can take this slot over. [font=Courier New]HP[/font], [font=Courier New]MP[/font] or [font=Courier New]SP[/font] for a health, magicka or stamina emergency; [font=Courier New]Other[/font] for the soul gem, low ammo and drowning prompts; [font=Courier New]Any[/font] for all of them; [font=Courier New]None[/font] to leave the slot alone[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]bSkipEquipped[/font] — Skip anything already in your hands, so the slot shows you an alternative instead. On by default; [font=Courier New]bSkipEquipped = 0[/font] lets a slot show a weapon you are holding (to double-tap it into the other hand, say)[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]bRemembrance[/font] — Swap back: when you press this slot to equip something, what it took off waits under the same key for a while (see Steadiness below). On by default; [font=Courier New]bRemembrance = 0[/font] turns it off for this slot[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]iPriority[/font] — Which slots get first pick of the good options. Higher fills first[/*]
[/list]

Here is the first slot of the first page in the default setup. It holds the weapon you are [i]not[/i] holding, gets first pick, and hands itself over to a healing potion when your health drops dangerously low:

[code][Page0.Slot0]
sClassification = WeaponsAny
bWildcardsEnabled = true
bOverridesEnabled = HP
iPriority = 7[/code]

[b][color=#cc9933]The default pages[/color][/b]

By default Huginn comes with two pages: [b]Huginn[/b] and [b]Kit[/b].

[b]Huginn page[/b]

The first page, [b]Huginn[/b], gives every key a job. Each key shows the best thing for that job that is not already in your hands:

[list]
[*][b]1 — Weapon[/b] — The weapon you're not holding — the bow while the sword is out. Swap, and the sword waits on this key, so one key toggles between two weapons[/*]
[*][b]2 — Attack magic[/b] — Damage spells and damage scrolls for the target[/*]
[*][b]3 — Heal[/b] — Healing spells and health potions[/*]
[*][b]4 — Defend[/b] — Wards, armour spells, resist potions[/*]
[*][b]5 — Buff[/b] — Fortify, invisibility, muffle[/*]
[*][b]6 — Potion[/b] — Whatever potion suits the moment[/*]
[*][b]7 — Situational[/b] — Anything, plus the quieter prompts: arrows running low, a soul gem, waterbreathing[/*]
[*][b]8 — Wildcard[/b] — The best of everything else, and now and then something new[/*]
[/list]

Emergencies always land on the same keys: health on 1, magicka on 2, stamina on 3 (see Emergencies — [Overrides] below to change this).

[b]Kit page[/b]

The second page, [b]Kit[/b], is for between fights: potions, food, scrolls, utility spells, summons, arrows and crafting gear, one kind per key.

[b][color=#cc9933]Template pages[/color][/b]

[font=Courier New]configs/templates/[/font] has six ready-made layouts:

[list]
[*]Battlemage[/*]
[*]Paladin[/*]
[*]Pure Mage[/*]
[*]Stealth Archer[/*]
[*]Summoner[/*]
[*]Survivalist[/*]
[/list]

Each keeps the same emergency keys, so switching never moves "heal me". To use one, delete [font=Courier New][Pages][/font] and every [font=Courier New][PageN][/font] / [font=Courier New][PageN.SlotM][/font] section in [font=Courier New]Huginn.ini[/font], paste the template in their place, and type [font=Courier New]hg reload[/font].

One thing worth knowing about emergencies: a slot set to [font=Courier New]Other[/font] will [i]not[/i] take health, magicka or stamina emergencies. That is deliberate — it keeps one slot free for the quieter prompts, so a soul gem or low-ammo warning still gets through when your health is also dropping.

[b][color=#cc9933]What can fill a slot[/color][/b]

[font=Courier New]sClassification[/font] is the one setting you'll change most, so it's worth reading this list. There are two ways to think about a slot: [b]by the job you want done[/b], or [b]by the kind of thing you want in it[/b].

[b]By job.[/b] These mix everything you own together — a spell, a potion, and a scroll can all compete for the same slot, and Huginn picks whichever fits the moment best.

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]DamageAny[/font] — Anything that hurts something: attack spells, damage scrolls, poisons, and your weapons[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]DamageMagic[/font] — Attack magic only: damage spells and damage scrolls — no weapons, no poisons[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]HealingAny[/font] — Healing spells, healing scrolls, and health potions[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]BuffsAny[/font] — Things you use [i]before[/i] the fight: armor and cloak spells, invisibility, muffle, and fortify potions[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]DefensiveAny[/font] — Wards, armor spells, and resist fire/frost/shock/magic/poison potions[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]SummonsAny[/font] — Conjuration — summoned creatures, raised undead, and bound weapons[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Utility[/font] — Everything else useful: candlelight, detect life, telekinesis, unlock, waterbreathing, cure disease and cure poison[/*]
[/list]

[b]By kind.[/b] These accept only one category of thing, which makes a page predictable — the same slot always holds the same sort of item.

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]SpellsAny[/font] — Any spell you know[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]SpellsDestruction[/font] — Destruction spells only[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]SpellsRestoration[/font] — Restoration spells only[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]SpellsConjuration[/font] — Conjuration spells only[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]SpellsIllusion[/font] — Illusion spells only[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]SpellsAlteration[/font] — Alteration spells only[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]ScrollsAny[/font] — Any scroll[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]PotionsAny[/font] — Any potion — restore, resist, fortify, cure, and poisons. Not food or drink[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]PoisonsAny[/font] — Poisons only[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]FoodAny[/font] — Food[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]AlcoholAny[/font] — Ale, mead, wine, skooma[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]WeaponsAny[/font] — Any weapon, including staves[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]WeaponsMelee[/font] — Swords, axes, maces, daggers[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]WeaponsRanged[/font] — Bows, crossbows, staves[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]AmmoAny[/font] — Arrows and bolts[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]ApparelAny[/font] — Gear that fortifies smithing, enchanting or alchemy — it only comes up at a workstation[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Regular[/font] — No restriction — anything at all can land here[/*]
[/list]

A few things to note:

[list]
[*][b][font=Courier New]Regular[/font] is the catch-all.[/b] Use it for slots you want Huginn to fill freely, and as overflow after your specific slots. If you typo a classification, Huginn falls back to [font=Courier New]Regular[/font] and notes it in the log.[/*]
[*][b]Weapons count as damage.[/b] A [font=Courier New]DamageAny[/font] slot can serve you a sword. If you want attack magic only there, use [font=Courier New]DamageMagic[/font].[/*]
[*][b]Held items are skipped.[/b] Every slot leaves out what you are already holding ([font=Courier New]bSkipEquipped[/font], on by default), which is what makes a weapon key show your [i]other[/i] weapon.[/*]
[*][b]Magic wands and staves are ranged.[/b] They match [font=Courier New]WeaponsRanged[/font], not the spell classifications.[/*]
[*][b]The names have short forms.[/b] [font=Courier New]damage[/font], [font=Courier New]healing[/font], [font=Courier New]buffs[/font], [font=Courier New]melee[/font], [font=Courier New]destruction[/font], [font=Courier New]drinks[/font], [font=Courier New]any[/font] and so on all work, and case doesn't matter.[/*]
[/list]

[b][color=#cc9933]Keybindings[/color][/b]

Huginn uses DirectInput scancodes for key activation. Only single keypresses are supported at this time.

[code]iSlot1Key = 2           ; the "1" key
iPreviousPageKey = 12
iNextPageKey = 13
iToggleWidgetKey = 45   ; 0 to unbind[/code]

Tap equips to the right hand, double-tap to the left hand, hold to both hands.

[b][color=#cc9933]Emergencies — [Overrides][/color][/b]

Most of the time Huginn quietly ranks your options. Sometimes something is urgent enough that it should stop suggesting and just put the answer on your key. That is an override: it seizes a slot, ignoring the normal ranking, until the emergency passes.

A slot only accepts an override if you let it. That is the [font=Courier New]bOverridesEnabled[/font] setting on the slot — [font=Courier New]HP[/font], [font=Courier New]MP[/font], [font=Courier New]SP[/font], [font=Courier New]Other[/font], [font=Courier New]Any[/font], or [font=Courier New]None[/font]. In the shipped layout, health takes [font=Courier New]Page0.Slot0[/font] (key 1), magicka takes [font=Courier New]Slot1[/font] (key 2), stamina takes [font=Courier New]Slot2[/font] (key 3), and everything else takes [font=Courier New]Slot6[/font] (key 7).

[list]
[*][b]Health[/b] — fires when health drops below 35%. Best health potion; failing that, a healing spell you can cast right now[/*]
[*][b]Magicka[/b] — fires when magicka drops below 35%. Best magicka potion; failing that, a restore spell you can afford[/*]
[*][b]Stamina[/b] — fires when stamina drops below 35%. Best stamina potion; failing that, a restore spell you can afford[/*]
[*][b]Weapon charge[/b] — fires when an enchanted weapon or staff falls below 25% charge. A filled soul gem[/*]
[*][b]Low ammo[/b] — fires when you are down to 10 arrows or bolts. The best ammo you are carrying[/*]
[*][b]Drowning[/b] — fires when you are underwater with no waterbreathing. Waterbreathing potion[/*]
[/list]

Each one has its own on/off switch — [font=Courier New]bEnableCriticalHealth[/font], [font=Courier New]bEnableWeaponCharge[/font], [font=Courier New]bEnableDrowning[/font] and so on. Turn off what you don't want. [font=Courier New]bHealthSpellFallback[/font] and its magicka and stamina twins turn off the spell fallback.

[b]Same key, or where it already is.[/b] By default health, magicka and stamina emergencies always use their own key, so "heal me" is muscle memory. If you'd rather nothing moved, set [font=Courier New]bPinHealthToSlot[/font], [font=Courier New]bPinMagickaToSlot[/font] or [font=Courier New]bPinStaminaToSlot[/font] to [font=Courier New]false[/font]: then, when the potion is already on another key, that key pulses instead. Arrows, soul gems and waterbreathing always work that second way.

[b]Thresholds and the release gap.[/b] Every emergency has a threshold and a hysteresis. The threshold is where it starts; the hysteresis is how far back you have to climb before it lets go. Health is [font=Courier New]0.35[/font] and [font=Courier New]0.15[/font], so it fires below 35% and releases above 50%. That gap is deliberate — without it a potion would flash on and off your key every time your health wobbled around a single number. Widen the gap if it lets go too eagerly; narrow it if it lingers.

Vital thresholds are fractions ([font=Courier New]0.35[/font] = 35%). Ammo is a plain count ([font=Courier New]10[/font] arrows, releasing at 25).

[font=Courier New]fMinOverrideDurationMs[/font] is a floor on the whole thing: once an emergency takes a slot, it holds it for at least 2 seconds no matter what, so nothing can flicker.

[b]Impure potions.[/b] [font=Courier New]bAllowImpurePotions = true[/font] lets Huginn fall back to something with a nasty side effect — skooma, say — when there is nothing clean left. It is a last resort, never a first pick.

[b]Soul gems have two switches, and they are separate on purpose.[/b] [font=Courier New]bEnableWeaponCharge[/font] here controls the "your weapon is nearly dead" prompt. [font=Courier New]bEnableSoulGemRecharge[/font] under [font=Courier New][Candidates][/font] controls whether gems show up in ordinary recommendations at all. Turning off the second one does [i]not[/i] stop the emergency prompt. Turn off both to stop seeing soul gems entirely.

[quote]Not to be confused with [font=Courier New]Huginn_Overrides.ini[/font], which is a different thing entirely — that file fixes how modded items get [i]categorised[/i]. See Classification Overrides below.[/quote]

[b][color=#cc9933]Algorithm configuration[/color][/b]

These settings are the ones that decide how Huginn makes up its mind. Tuning them is guesswork even with a good sense of how it works, so change one thing at a time and see what happens.

Fiddle at your own risk. If you want to know what each number actually feeds into, [url=https://github.com/LandingCrew/huginn/blob/main/docs/README.md]the full write-up is here[/url].

[b]What Huginn pays attention to — [ContextWeights][/b]

This is the section that decides [i]what matters right now[/i]. Each setting is how loudly one condition shouts. Raise a number and Huginn reacts more strongly to that situation; set it to [font=Courier New]0[/font] and it stops caring about it altogether.

The loud ones are the emergencies you can see happening to you:

[code]fWeightOnFire = 8.0
fWeightFrozen = 8.0
fWeightShocked = 8.0
fWeightPoisoned = 6.0
fWeightUnderwater = 10.0
fWeightFallingHigh = 8.0
fWeightLookingAtLock = 10.0[/code]

The rest sit on a 0-to-1 scale and cover the ordinary run of play: being in combat, facing several enemies, an enemy mid-cast, sneaking, standing at a forge or enchanter or alchemy lab, fighting undead or daedra or a dragon, being out of ammo, having no weapon drawn.

Two of them are worth knowing about because they exist to stop things disappearing:

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]fWeightBuffPotion = 0.15[/font] is a constant low hum that keeps fortify and resist potions visible at all. Without it they would never score highly enough to be offered, and so Huginn would never get the chance to learn that you like them.[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]fWeightSoulGem = 0.15[/font] does the same for filled soul gems, so you can see one even when you are not carrying a drained enchanted weapon. Set it to [font=Courier New]0[/font] to only ever see gems when a weapon actually needs one.[/*]
[/list]

The three [font=Courier New]SmoothingExponent[/font] settings shape how sharply the health, magicka and stamina curves ramp up as the bar drains. [font=Courier New]1.0[/font] is a straight line, [font=Courier New]2.0[/font] (the default) stays calm until things get genuinely bad, [font=Courier New]3.0[/font] more so.

[b]Ranking — [Scoring] and [Favorites][/b]

[font=Courier New][Scoring][/font] is the machinery that turns "what matters now" plus "what do you like" into a final order. Most of it you can leave alone. The parts worth touching:

[list]
[*][b][font=Courier New]fLambdaMin[/font] / [font=Courier New]fLambdaMax[/font][/b] — how much weight Huginn gives your learned habits versus the situation in front of you. It starts near [font=Courier New]fLambdaMin[/font] while it barely knows you, and rises to [font=Courier New]fLambdaMax[/font] once it does. Raise them to have it lean harder on your habits; lower them to keep it more reactive.[/*]
[*][b]The bonus settings[/b] — small nudges for combinations that make sense together: matching arrows to your bow, a shield spell with a melee weapon, silver against undead, a staff when your magicka is low. Adjust or zero out individually.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]iTopNCandidates = 10[/font][/b] — how many options get considered in detail each update.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]fMinimumUtility[/font][/b] — the bar an option has to clear to be shown at all. Raise it if you are seeing filler you don't want.[/*]
[/list]

[font=Courier New][Favorites][/font] decides what your favourites flag means. [font=Courier New]Boost[/font] (the default) pushes favourited items up the order. [font=Courier New]Suppress[/font] does the opposite and hides them, on the grounds that they are already one keypress away in your favourites menu. [font=Courier New]Off[/font] ignores the flag.

[b]Learning and trying new things — [Wildcards] and [Learning][/b]

Huginn can only learn that you like something if it offers it to you occasionally. That is what wildcards are: now and then a lower-ranked option takes a slot, so it gets a chance to be picked.

Wildcards never touch your top slot — that always holds the genuine best pick — and they get rarer the more you care about a slot. [font=Courier New]fBaseProbability[/font] and [font=Courier New]fMaxProbability[/font] control how often they appear; the two cooldown settings stop the same slot churning. You can also turn them off per slot with [font=Courier New]bWildcardsEnabled = false[/font].

[font=Courier New][Learning][/font] covers the other half: Huginn also watches what you equip [i]outside[/i] of it, through the vanilla inventory, the favourites menu, or a vanilla hotkey. Reaching past Huginn for something is a strong signal, and the reward settings say how strong. [font=Courier New]bLearnFromExternalEquips = false[/font] turns this off if you would rather it only learn from your use of its own keys.

[b]Steadiness — [SlotLocker][/b]

A recommendation that changes the instant before you press the key is worse than a merely decent one that stays put. When a slot fills, Huginn briefly locks it so it cannot be swapped out from under your thumb.

[font=Courier New]fLockDurationMs = 3000[/font] is that hold. Raise it for a calmer, slower display; lower it for one that reacts faster. [font=Courier New]0[/font] disables locking entirely. Emergencies are allowed to break a lock, which is what [font=Courier New]bOverridesBreakLock[/font] and [font=Courier New]iImmediateBreakPriority[/font] govern. [font=Courier New]hg unlock[/font] clears every lock immediately.

[b]Swap back.[/b] Press a key that puts a weapon, spell or scroll in your hand, and whatever was in that hand appears under the [i]same[/i] key, labelled "Swap Back", for [font=Courier New]fRemembranceDurationMs[/font] (15000 = 15 seconds). Press it to put the old item back; the key then returns to normal. Arrows work the same way, arrows for arrows: switch from Iron to Steel Arrows with a key and the Iron Arrows wait on it. Only a swap counts; equipping into an empty hand or quiver leaves nothing to swap back to. It works from Huginn's hotkeys and Huginn's own Wheeler wheels, not from the inventory menu or favourites. It is not a recommendation, so swapping back teaches Huginn nothing.

To turn it off for one slot, add [font=Courier New]bRemembrance = 0[/font] to that slot's section:

[code][Page0.Slot3]
bRemembrance = 0[/code]

To turn it off everywhere, set [font=Courier New]fRemembranceDurationMs = 0[/font] under [font=Courier New][SlotLocker][/font]. Either way, [font=Courier New]hg reload[/font] applies it without restarting.

A remembered item pulses for the last few seconds before it goes. When it doesn't fit the key you pressed — a spell took your dagger off, and the dagger now sits on the attack-magic key — it only stays for [font=Courier New]fRemembranceMismatchDurationMs[/font] (5 seconds). Set [font=Courier New]sRemembranceTarget = Job[/font] to send it to a key where it does fit instead (the dagger goes to your weapon key).

[font=Courier New]bFillJobKeysFromRegular[/font] covers the odd moment a job key would be empty while an "anything" key holds something that fits it: turn it on and the item moves to the job key. It is off by default, because it moves things between keys.

[b]Spells you can't currently cast — [Candidates][/b]

[font=Courier New]sUncastableSpellPolicy[/font] decides what happens to spells you cannot afford right now:

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]Disallow[/font] (the default) — hide them; a key you cannot use is wasted space[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Penalize[/font] — keep them, ranked lower the further out of reach they are[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Allow[/font] — rank them normally, magicka be damned[/*]
[/list]

[font=Courier New]bEnableSoulGemRecharge[/font] belongs to this section too — see the soul gem note under Emergencies above.

[size=3][b]Classification Overrides[/b][/size]

[b]Configuration Location:[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/Huginn_Overrides.ini[/font]

Huginn classifies vanilla items and spells automatically. Modded potions, foods, and spells sometimes need a hint. This file supplies it:

[code][Item:Battlemage's Elixir]
type = BuffPotion
tags = FortifyMagicSchool,FortifyHealth

[Spell:Scroll of Bolide]
type = Damage
tags = Fire,Ranged[/code]

The [font=Courier New]Item:[/font] / [font=Courier New]Spell:[/font] prefix matters. This one file covers both items and spells, and some type and tag names mean something in each, so a section without a prefix is tried against [i]both[/i] — and can quietly reclassify a potion and a spell that happen to share a name. Prefix every section. The file itself lists every type and tag you can use, for items and for spells.

Changes take effect on [font=Courier New]hg rebuild[/font] — no game restart needed, and [font=Courier New]hg reload[/font] is not enough (it reloads settings, not classifications).

[size=3][b]dMenu UI Controls[/b][/size]

[b]Configuration Location:[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/dmenu/customSettings/ini/Huginn.ini[/font]

This controls the in-game Intuition widget's appearance and, on a debug build, its debugging output. dMenu owns these settings exclusively — position, opacity, scale, display mode, and the widget on/off switch — and you edit them from Huginn's panel in the dMenu UI rather than by hand. Without dMenu installed, the widget uses its built-in defaults.

The panel also has four buttons: [b]Show/Hide Widget[/b], [b]Reset Learned Weights[/b] (makes Huginn forget everything it has learned about your preferences), [b]Reset to Defaults[/b], and [b]Reload INI[/b].

Two controls turn the widget off:

[list]
[*][b]Enable Widget[/b] — the checkbox. A saved setting; off means the widget is never built. Leave it on unless you want it gone for good.[/*]
[*][b]Show/Hide Widget[/b] — the button. An ephemeral runtime toggle: same as the hotkey, not saved, back to shown on the next load.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Optional Extension[/b][/size]

[size=3][b]WheelerAPI[/b][/size]

Huginn can connect to the Wheeler plugin and drive its wheels directly, so each page becomes a wheel and each slot a wheel entry.

This needs [url=https://github.com/LandingCrew/wheelerAPI]wheelerAPI[/url], available here as an optional download. It adds the connection point Huginn talks to, which the original Wheeler does not have on its own — without it Huginn simply stays disconnected and everything else keeps working as normal.

It also carries a few stability patches, and adds soul gem support.

[b][color=#cc9933]Install[/color][/b]

[list=1]
[*]Download and install the original [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/97345]Wheeler[/url] mod — its supporting files are still needed.[/*]
[*]Install wheelerAPI after Wheeler and let it overwrite.[/*]
[/list]

[quote]You do [b]not[/b] need [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/132074]Wheeler CTD-Fix[/url]. wheelerAPI already includes those fixes.[/quote]

[b][color=#cc9933]Compatibility[/color][/b]

wheelerAPI works alongside:

[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/161698]Wheeler - Show in UI[/url][/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/100043]Dragonborn Reskin - Wheeler[/url][/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/100692]Wheeler Icon Embellishment - Naturally Exquisite Refinement[/url][/*]
[/list]

If you would rather not use Huginn's Wheeler variant, [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/167380]WHEELER - Refined[/url] works too. You keep the labels under each entry, but give up two conveniences: Huginn loses track of its wheels once you rearrange them, so it will not remember where you dragged them — [font=Courier New]sWheelPosition[/font] stays in charge — and if another mod shuffles them mid-game, Huginn cannot put them back on its own.

[b][color=#cc9933]Usage[/color][/b]

Huginn attempts to connect to Wheeler automatically and manages its own wheels based on the [font=Courier New][Wheeler][/font] section of [font=Courier New]Huginn.ini[/font]. Two behaviours are worth knowing before you rearrange anything:

[list]
[*][b]Opening Wheeler jumps you to Huginn's wheels.[/b] Open Wheeler on any wheel that isn't Huginn's and it takes you straight to Huginn's first one. That stings most if you have moved one of your own wheels to the very front — Wheeler opens on it, Huginn moves you along, and the only way back is to scroll. You get an in-game notice the first time it happens. Set [font=Courier New]bAutoFocusOnOpen = false[/font] to stay on whichever wheel you opened.[/*]
[*][b]Where you drag Huginn's wheels sticks until you quit.[/b] Rearrange them in Wheeler's edit mode and they will still be there after you load a save. Two catches: restarting Skyrim puts them back where [font=Courier New]sWheelPosition[/font] says, and they always come back as one group, so if you had tucked your own wheels in among them they will not stay in between. To set the order deliberately, change [font=Courier New]sWheelPosition[/font] and run [font=Courier New]hg reload[/font]. None of this applies with WHEELER - Refined, which cannot tell Huginn where its wheels ended up — there [font=Courier New]sWheelPosition[/font] always wins.[/*]
[/list]

The rest of the [font=Courier New][Wheeler][/font] settings:

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]sWheelPosition[/font] — Where Huginn's wheels sit in the rotation — [font=Courier New]First[/font], [font=Courier New]Last[/font], or a number[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]bAutoFocusOnOverride[/font] — Jump to Huginn's wheel when an emergency fires. On by default[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]iAutoFocusMinPriority[/font] — How serious an emergency has to be before that jump happens. Drowning counts as 50, low magicka 70, low health 100 — so the default of 50 jumps for anything, and raising it to 100 jumps only when you are about to die[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]sPostActivationPolicy[/font] — What the slot does after you use what was in it. [font=Courier New]Backfill[/font] puts the next-best thing there, [font=Courier New]Sticky[/font] leaves the used item showing until the situation changes, [font=Courier New]Empty[/font] clears the slot and shows "Equipped"[/*]
[/list]

[b][color=#cc9933]Wheel entry labels — [Subtexts][/color][/b]

Wheeler entries can carry a small line of text under the item name, explaining why it is there. [font=Courier New][Subtexts][/font] controls those: whether to mark wildcard picks and emergencies, whether to show the lock countdown, and whether to show Huginn's one-line reason for the pick. [font=Courier New]fOffsetX[/font] and [font=Courier New]fOffsetY[/font] nudge the label's position in pixels if it collides with your Wheeler skin.

Both wheelerAPI and WHEELER - Refined support these labels. If they never appear, check [font=Courier New]Huginn.log[/font] for the line beginning [font=Courier New]Connected to Wheeler API[/font] — it tells you what Huginn found.

[size=4][b]Mod Compatibility[/b][/size]

[size=3][b]Lighting Mods[/b][/size]

Huginn estimates how dark it is from the light level the game itself reports (measured on your character — the same value guards use to spot you sneaking). When it is dark, Night Eye and light spells such as Candlelight and Magelight come forward, and they drop back once one is active or a torch is out.

Lighting mods change that value, so how often this happens depends on your load order. With a dark lighting setup, caves and moonless nights count as dark. With a bright one, or the vanilla game, the light may never fall low enough and Night Eye will not be suggested for darkness.

To turn the darkness suggestions off entirely, set [font=Courier New]fWeightDarkness = 0[/font] under [font=Courier New][ContextWeights][/font].
